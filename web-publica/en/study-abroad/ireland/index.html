Why Ireland

  • You can choose between living with a family or boarding.
  • Very close to Spain, with no visa and only one hour’s difference.
  • Transition Year, at schools that offer it, is a year designed for growing and discovering interests.

Academic, sporting and artistic development

Team sport, creativity, music and career exploration, especially during Transition Year. Each school has its own offer.

The education system, explained

Junior Cycle
The first three years of secondary school. Students start at around 12 or 13 and finish with an exam at around 15 or 16.
Transition Year
An optional year between the Junior Cycle and the Leaving Certificate, with projects, work experience and activities to discover interests. Most schools offer it, not all, and each designs its own.
Leaving Certificate
The last two years, with a final exam. Irish is a compulsory subject unless there is an exemption.

A term or a full year?

Some schools accept students by the term and others for the full year. Compare a term and a full year

Accommodation and daily life

With a host family, students live their routine and attend a day school; when boarding, life revolves around the school. It is worth checking travel times and whether the boarding house is open at weekends.

It may suit your child if they…

Wants to gain confidence, improve their English, discover new interests and have an international experience close to Spain.

Worth knowing

Paperwork
Ireland is in the European Union: Spanish students do not need a visa.
Transition Year
It is optional and not every school offers it. If you are interested, we take it into account when choosing a school.
Coming back
Age, content and continuity need checking, especially if they do Transition Year.
Recognition in Spain
No recognition is needed to join any year of Primary or ESO in Spain; for Bachillerato, it is. We review each case.

Questions about Ireland

What is Transition Year?

An optional year, usually at around 15 or 16, with projects, work experience and activities to discover interests. Each school organises it in its own way.

Will they have to study Irish?

Irish is compulsory in the system unless there is an exemption. We explain how each school applies it.

Host family or boarding?

It depends on their personality, age and what you are looking for. We discuss it with you before choosing a school.
