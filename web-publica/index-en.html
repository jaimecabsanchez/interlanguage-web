Frequently asked questions

What ages is the programme for?

From Early Years to Secondary. Each stage has its own club, with goals and activities suited to their age.

How are the groups organised?

By school stage, in small groups that open from 8 pupils, so everyone gets real time to speak.

Are the teachers really native speakers?

Yes. 100% of our teaching staff are native speakers, specifically trained to teach children and young people, not just to speak the language.

When do classes start and finish?

The programme runs from October to May, following the school calendar, in the afternoon at your child's own school.

How much does it cost and what's included?

At Colegio Sagrado Corazón, €65 a month plus €35 a year for materials. If your school is a different one, get in touch and we'll confirm the terms.

Is it right for your child's age and stage?

Tell us their year group and what you're looking for, and we'll guide you with no commitment.

Programmes

School ages and boarding ages differ. One preparatory school offers boarding from 7 to 13; other schools extend to age 18. Entry depends on each school.

  • Full school yearThe usual option.Ask for guidance about the full school year
  • One termAt some schools, with limited places; others do not offer short stays.Ask for guidance about one term
  • Two termsAt some schools, with limited places; others do not offer short stays.Ask for guidance about two terms
Admission

Each school decides on admission: some work from reports, an interview or a taster day, with no entrance exam, and others ask for an English assessment. Several offer English language support (EAL), sometimes at an extra cost.

School

Independent co-educational schools, with day pupils and boarders. At most of them you can study from primary to age 18; one is preparatory only (ages 4 to 13).

Education system (general information): England and Scotland have different education systems, each with its own curriculum and exams.
Accommodation

Boarding at the school itself, full-time or part-time depending on the school. At the preparatory school, boarding is for ages 7 to 13; other schools start in Year 5 or Year 7. The entry year is checked with the school. Several organise weekend activities. Some require international boarders to have a guardian: a responsible adult resident in the UK.

Day to day

Boarders have evening and weekend activities at several schools. Sport and other activities (golf or horse riding at some, for example) vary a lot from one school to another.

Questions
  • Does the school require a guardian for international boarders?
  • Which weekends is the boarding house open?
  • Is there English language support, and does it cost extra?
  • Which school year is your child's equivalent?
  • Does it accept one term or only the full school year?

Programmes

For young people aged 10 to 18. The length of stay and the start date are worked out with each school.

Admission

The English level and admission conditions are reviewed with each school before a place is proposed.

School

Secondary schools, most of them co-educational; there are also boys-only and girls-only schools. Some are boarding schools with a religious tradition (Catholic, Church of Ireland or Quaker) and others are day schools, including community and multi-denominational colleges.

Accommodation

Two formats: boarding at the school, for 5, 5½ or 7 days depending on the school, or a day school with a host family. Some boarding schools have closed weekends, and on those days other accommodation is needed.

Day to day

Campuses with boarding life and activities that vary from one school to another: some stand out for team sport and others for music or culture.

Frequently asked questions

What families usually ask us

What is the minimum age?

In the United Kingdom, one preparatory school offers boarding from 7 to 13; others start in later school years and extend to 18. In Ireland the range is 10 to 18, with the entry year depending on the school. For the United States, ask about your case.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the destination, the length and the school. Before deciding, check what the proposal includes and what is paid separately, such as travel, transfers, English support or accommodation during closures, where applicable.

When should we start?

There is no single date: it depends on the school and on the option. That is why the first step is to talk.

Is it possible to go for just one term?

In the United Kingdom, some schools accept one or two terms, with limited places; others only the full school year. For Ireland, check the length with each school.

What if we do not know which destination or length to choose?

Tell us their age, school year and what you are looking for. We guide you so you can compare the options, with no obligation.

Does asking for guidance commit me to anything?

No. It is a conversation so you have the information. The decision is yours.
