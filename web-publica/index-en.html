Frequently asked questions

What ages is the programme for?+

From Early Years to Secondary. Each stage has its own club, with goals and activities suited to their age.

How are the groups organised?+

By school stage, in small groups that open from 8 pupils, so everyone gets real time to speak.

Are the teachers really native speakers?+

Yes. 100% of our teaching staff are native speakers, specifically trained to teach children and young people, not just to speak the language.

When do classes start and finish?+

The programme runs from October to May, following the school calendar, in the afternoon at your child's own school.

How much does it cost and what's included?+

At Colegio Sagrado Corazón, €65 a month plus €35 a year for materials. If your school is a different one, get in touch and we'll confirm the terms.

Is it right for your child's age and stage?

Tell us their year group and what you're looking for, and we'll guide you with no commitment.

Frequently asked questions

What families ask us

What is a typical day like?

Every morning with the host family, breakfast and off to school like anyone else. Lessons in English, activities in the afternoon and weekends with the family or other international students. They live like a local pupil, with the whole routine.

What if they fall behind at school?

We review the academic path beforehand so the year abroad adds up rather than setting them back. We work with schools that recognise subjects, we coordinate with the school in Spain on full-year programmes, and we plan the return so they rejoin without gaps.

What if the host family does not work out?

Host families go through a selection and verification process, and have hosted international students before. Before the trip you have a video call to meet them. If something does not fit once there, there is a procedure to change family at no extra cost.

What about safety?

Programmes include international medical insurance for the stay, a local coordinator for anything unexpected and an emergency contact in Spain. Your child is never on their own, however far away they are.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the destination, the length and the school. We work it out with you in the first conversation, with no obligation.

When should we start?

There is no single date: it depends on the school and on the programme dates. Every case is different, which is why the first step is to talk.

Are you considering it for next year?

Tell us their age, their level and what you have in mind. We will tell you honestly whether it fits and what the options are.
