Frequently asked questions

What ages is the programme for?

For ages 6 to 12, with groups adapted to each stage. The year groups offered depend on each school.

How are the groups organised?

By school stage, in small groups that open from 8 pupils, so everyone gets real time to speak.

Are the teachers really native speakers?

Yes. All our teaching staff are native speakers, specifically trained to teach children and young people, not just to speak the language.

When do classes start and finish?

The programme runs from October to May, following the school calendar, in the afternoon at your child's own school.

How much does it cost and what's included?

At Colegio Sagrado Corazón, €65 a month plus €35 a year for materials. If your school is a different one, email us at info@interlanguage.es and we'll confirm the terms.

Is it right for your child's age and stage?

Explore the groups. If your school has sent you a registration link, use that link to enrol.

Duration and budget

One to four weeks. Ask for prices for your school and dates. The quote should detail accommodation, activities and supplements.

Transfers and some activities cost extra. Ask about the cost of an extra night too.

Accommodation, care and keeping in touch

In the boarding house

Single or shared rooms, with shared bathrooms cleaned daily. A team lives with the students 24 hours a day, looks after passports, money and medicines, and gives medication on time.

Arrival and transfers

Staff meet them at airport arrivals and take them to school by minibus from Edinburgh, Heathrow or Gatwick. The transfer costs extra.

The journey

Ask how flights are organised and what accompaniment is available before booking.

Keeping in touch

Ask about the schedule and channel for contact with home before booking.

Phones

Not used in lessons, activities, meals or overnight. Staff keep them safe; families can request otherwise in writing.

Clear rules

No alcohol, smoking or vaping. Boys and girls only mix in common areas. Zero tolerance of bullying.

Why this camp

English stops being a school subject and becomes the language of the whole day.

Project-based English

Every morning from 9:00 to 12:30, in small groups with qualified teachers. They learn by presenting, creating and working in teams.

Friends from around the world

They live alongside boys and girls from more than 20 countries. English is what they share.

Afternoons they choose

Sport, outdoor adventure, golf, horse riding, art or discovering the country: each student picks their plan.

Care around the clock

A team lives in the boarding house with the students: they know each one and look after them day and night.

Two English programmes

Every student has 15 hours of English a week. Older students can choose the leadership programme.

General English

Frequently asked questions

From what age can they go?

From age 8 at Strathallan School and from 10 at the other three schools, up to 17.

What level of English do they need?

No particular level: lessons are in small groups by level. The leadership programme requires B1.

Who do they share a room with?

They can choose a single or shared room. Bathrooms are shared and cleaned daily.

Are there many Spanish students?

The camps welcome students from more than 20 countries, so English is everyone’s language.

Can they use their phone?

Yes, in their free time. Not in lessons, activities, meals or overnight.

What if they fall ill?

The boarding team looks after them and lets you know. That is why medical insurance is compulsory.

How do they get from the airport?

Minibus transfers run from Edinburgh, Heathrow and Gatwick, at extra cost. Staff wait at arrivals with a sign.

When should we book?

Ask about availability and payment deadlines for your chosen school.

Shall we find their camp?

Tell us their age, their level and the dates you have in mind, and we will suggest the school that fits best. Our guidance is free.

Highlights

  • Lessons, study and boarding life at the same school.
  • A wide range of academic options and activities.
  • Close to Spain: just 1 hour behind.

Bear in mind

  • Boarding school is a significant investment: tuition, accommodation and extras.
  • Some schools require a guardian, and closures and holidays need planning.
  • Places for a single term and English support depend on the school.

How school works

Type of school
We focus on independent schools, day or boarding. England and Scotland have different systems.
Qualifications
England: GCSEs or IGCSEs and A levels. Scotland: National 5, Highers and Advanced Highers. Some schools offer the IB.
School calendar
Usually three terms: September to July in England and August to June in Scotland.
Returning to Spain
British Years do not correspond directly to Spanish school years. Before choosing, we check the stage and the subjects.

Frequently asked questions

What families usually ask us

Will they be safe and well looked after?

We explain how each school organises the care of its pupils and who their point of contact will be there. If the school requires a guardian, we help you arrange one.

What if they find it hard to settle in?

The first few days are usually the hardest. We prepare them before departure and, during the stay, provide the follow-up we agree on.

Will we be able to talk to them often?

Yes. Each school has its own rules on phones and times; we tell you about them before you choose.

Can they go for just one term?

Yes, but not at every school. In the United Kingdom, some accept one or two terms with limited places; in Ireland, terms and full school years.

When should we start?

Ideally one school year in advance: places can fill up. If you contact us later, we check which options remain.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the destination, the length of stay and the school. The proposal shows what is included and what is paid separately: travel, transfers or English support, for example.

What documents are needed?

It depends on the destination and the school: the admission application, reports from their current school and, in some cases, a visa and insurance. We tell you what each one asks for.

And when they come back to Spain?

Joining Primary or ESO does not require validation; for ESO or Bachillerato qualifications there are requirements. We check this before choosing a school.

Does asking for guidance commit us to anything?

No. It is a conversation so that you have the information. The decision is yours.
